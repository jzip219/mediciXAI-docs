Interpretation rule
-------------------

Benchmark Specification v1.0 is frozen. Future semantic changes require a new specification version. Implementation improvements that do not alter benchmark semantics may be incorporated into conforming implementations without changing the specification version.

Documentation freeze note
-------------------------

Benchmark Specification v1.0 remains frozen.

Documentation additions made after the initial freeze are limited to non-semantic usability improvements, including navigation enhancements and operator onboarding guidance.

These documentation updates do not modify benchmark formulas, requirements, conformance criteria, artifact definitions, or other normative specification semantics.
